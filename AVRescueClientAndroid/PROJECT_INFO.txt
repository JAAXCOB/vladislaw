AV Rescue Android 2.1.1.

Native Android phone application, not a desktop application and not a WebView copy of the site.

Implemented:
- unified AV Rescue login via bearer token;
- branded dark/red interface;
- client/executor role-aware dashboard;
- service area indicator;
- native order creation;
- GPS location;
- native order details;
- executor actions: accept, reject, en route, arrived, four vehicle photos, loading, transport and completion;
- order history;
- Russian Yandex Maps for pickup and destination;
- live executor location and ETA tracking;
- foreground online mode with background GPS, stationary-driver heartbeat and new-order polling;
- loud looping order alert that stops after accept/reject;
- server-synchronised five-minute acceptance countdown and automatic reassignment;
- executor service categories and service-aware order matching;
- client order cancellation;
- cash/card commission ledger and rating flow;
- logout;
- adaptive AV Rescue launcher icon.

Backend: https://av-rescue.ru
Package: ru.avrescue.app
Target SDK: 36
Version: 2.1.1 (22)
GLONASS: intentionally deferred to the next integration stage.
